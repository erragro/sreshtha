# Chapter I — Preliminary

## Section 2 — Definitions

"aggregator" means a digital intermediary or a marketplace for a buyer or user of a service to connect with the seller or the service provider, and includes any organisation or individual operating an online platform in the State that connects a platform-based gig worker to a customer;

"platform-based gig worker" means a person engaged in work or an activity outside of a traditional employer-employee relationship, using an online platform, in exchange for payment or reward, and includes a person engaged in the delivery of a service or in undertaking work in connection with such a platform;

"Board" means the Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Welfare Board constituted under section 4;

"Fund" means the Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Social Security and Welfare Fund constituted under section 8;

"Universal Account Number" or "UAN" means the unique identification number issued to a registered platform-based gig worker by the Board, either directly or through the eShram portal notified under the Code on Social Security, 2020.

# Chapter II — Registration

## Section 3 — Registration of every platform-based gig worker

Every platform-based gig worker working in Rajasthan shall be registered with the Board and receive a Universal Account Number.

The registration shall not depend on the duration for which the worker has been engaged on a platform, nor on whether the worker is engaged on multiple platforms.

Registration is free. It is a prerequisite for entitlement to benefits under any scheme notified by the Board.

# Chapter III — Board

## Section 4 — Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Welfare Board

The State Government shall, by notification, constitute a Board consisting of:
(a) the Minister-in-charge of Labour and Employment, as Chairperson;
(b) representatives of the State Government (Labour, Finance, IT departments);
(c) representatives of aggregators;
(d) representatives of registered gig-worker unions or associations;
(e) a representative of civil society working with unorganised labour;
(f) two women members with experience in workers' welfare.

## Section 5 — Functions of the Board

The Board shall:
(a) register every platform-based gig worker in the State;
(b) frame and administer social-security schemes for registered workers, including accident cover, health and disability protection, and old-age benefits;
(c) administer the Fund;
(d) redress grievances of platform-based gig workers concerning platform practices;
(e) publish an annual report on the Fund's inflow, outflow, and beneficiary count;
(f) do such other things as the State Government may notify.

# Chapter IV — Welfare Cess and Fund

## Section 7 — Welfare Cess

Every aggregator shall pay a welfare cess to the Board at such rate as the State Government may notify, being not less than one per cent and not more than two per cent of the value of every transaction between the aggregator and the platform-based gig worker.

The cess shall be paid in the time and manner prescribed. The receipts shall be credited to the Fund.

## Section 8 — Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Social Security and Welfare Fund

The State Government shall, by notification, constitute a Fund into which shall be credited:
(a) the welfare cess under section 7;
(b) grants or loans made by the Central or State Government;
(c) contributions from platform-based gig workers, if any, as may be prescribed;
(d) any other source as may be prescribed.

The Fund shall be used exclusively for the benefit of registered platform-based gig workers.

# Chapter V — Rights and Grievance Redressal

## Section 10 — Grievance Redressal

An aggregator shall establish an internal grievance-redressal system for platform-based gig workers.

If a grievance is not resolved within the time notified by the State Government (not exceeding thirty days), the aggrieved worker may escalate the grievance to the Board.

The Board shall dispose of every escalated grievance within ninety days of receipt of the escalation.

## Section 11 — Right to information about the platform

Every platform-based gig worker has the right to receive, in a language they can read, information about:
(a) the terms of the engagement with the aggregator;
(b) the calculation method for payment;
(c) any change in the terms or in the calculation method, with reasonable notice.
