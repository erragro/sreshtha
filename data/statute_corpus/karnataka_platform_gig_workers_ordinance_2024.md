# Chapter I — Preliminary

## Section 2 — Definitions

"aggregator" means a digital intermediary for a buyer or user of a service to connect with the seller or service provider and includes ride-sharing platforms, food and grocery delivery platforms, logistics services, e-marketplaces (both marketplace and inventory models), professional services, healthcare, travel and hospitality, and content and media services;

"platform-based gig worker" means a person engaged in work or activity outside of a traditional employer-employee relationship using an online platform, in exchange for payment;

"Board" means the Karnataka Platform-Based Gig Workers Welfare Board constituted under section 6;

"Fund" means the Karnataka Platform-Based Gig Workers Social Security and Welfare Fund constituted under section 12.

# Chapter II — Registration

## Section 4 — Registration of platform-based gig workers

Every platform-based gig worker working in the State of Karnataka shall be registered under the provisions of this Ordinance.

Every platform-based gig worker shall be entitled to be registered irrespective of the duration for which the worker has been on the platform, or the number of platforms on which the worker is engaged.

Registration shall be through the portal notified by the State Government. Registration is free of charge.

## Section 5 — Registration of aggregators

Every aggregator having operations in the State of Karnataka shall, within the time and manner prescribed, register with the Board.

# Chapter III — Board

## Section 6 — Karnataka Platform-Based Gig Workers Welfare Board

The State Government shall, by notification, constitute a Board to be known as the Karnataka Platform-Based Gig Workers Welfare Board.

The Board shall consist of representatives of the State Government, of aggregators registered under section 5, of civil-society organisations working in the sector, and of platform-based gig workers themselves, in the composition prescribed by the State Government.

## Section 8 — Functions of the Board

The Board shall:

(a) formulate and administer welfare schemes for registered platform-based gig workers;
(b) administer the Fund constituted under section 12;
(c) implement social-security schemes framed under the Code on Social Security, 2020 to the extent applicable to gig workers in Karnataka;
(d) redress grievances of platform-based gig workers arising out of platform practices, in coordination with the Grievance Redressal mechanism under section 15;
(e) do such other things as the State Government may notify.

# Chapter IV — Welfare Fee

## Section 11 — Welfare fee on aggregators

Every aggregator shall pay a welfare fee to the Board, at such rate as may be notified by the State Government, being not less than one per cent and not more than five per cent of the payout to a platform-based gig worker for a transaction on the aggregator's platform.

The welfare fee shall be paid on such basis and at such intervals as may be prescribed. The receipts shall be credited to the Fund constituted under section 12.

## Section 12 — Karnataka Platform-Based Gig Workers Social Security and Welfare Fund

The State Government shall, by notification, constitute a Fund to be known as the Karnataka Platform-Based Gig Workers Social Security and Welfare Fund.

Into the Fund shall be credited the welfare fee under section 11, any grants or loans made by the State Government or the Central Government, contributions received by the Board, and any other source as may be prescribed.

The Fund shall be used exclusively for the welfare of registered platform-based gig workers.

# Chapter V — Rights of Platform-Based Gig Workers

## Section 14 — Termination of contract with a platform-based gig worker

An aggregator shall not terminate the engagement of any platform-based gig worker without a valid reason, communicated in writing to the worker, and without providing a period of notice of at least fourteen days.

The reasons for termination shall be limited to those specified in the contract between the aggregator and the platform-based gig worker, or those permitted under the Code on Social Security, 2020 or any other law for the time being in force.

## Section 15 — Grievance Redressal

An aggregator shall have an internal grievance redressal mechanism, prescribed by the Board, for platform-based gig workers to raise complaints concerning platform practices.

Every unresolved grievance may be escalated to the Board within thirty days of the aggregator's response (or within fifteen days of the aggregator's inaction, whichever is earlier).

The Board shall dispose of every escalated grievance within ninety days of receipt.
