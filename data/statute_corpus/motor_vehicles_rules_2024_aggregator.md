# Applicability

These provisions apply to every aggregator operating in India that connects a driver-partner to a customer through an online platform, including ride-hailing, delivery of goods, and courier services.

An "aggregator" is a digital intermediary or marketplace facilitating a transaction between a driver-partner and a customer through an online platform, in exchange for a fee or a commission.

# Driver welfare obligations

## Insurance

Every aggregator shall obtain, at its cost, a health insurance policy for every driver-partner engaged on the platform, for a sum insured of at least Rs 5 lakh, renewable annually.

Every aggregator shall obtain, at its cost, a term insurance policy for every driver-partner, for a sum insured of at least Rs 10 lakh, covering death from any cause during the period of engagement.

Every aggregator shall obtain, at its cost, an accident insurance policy covering death or permanent disability of the driver-partner arising from any incident during the performance of work assigned by the aggregator.

The driver-partner shall be entitled to a copy of every policy document referenced above, in a language they can read, on request.

## Safety training

Every aggregator shall conduct or arrange for a mandatory safety-training programme for every driver-partner at least once every twelve months. The training shall cover road-safety practices, gender-sensitivity training, and grievance-redressal mechanisms available to the driver-partner.

## Working hours

An aggregator shall limit the daily active working hours of any driver-partner on the platform to twelve hours in a twenty-four-hour period. The aggregator shall not accept work requests from a driver-partner who has exceeded this cap in the preceding twenty-four hours until the driver-partner has had at least ten consecutive hours off the platform.

# Grievance redressal

Every aggregator shall designate a Grievance Redressal Officer whose contact details shall be prominently displayed within the aggregator's application.

Every driver-partner may file a complaint with the Grievance Redressal Officer regarding any matter arising from the engagement, including payment disputes, deactivation without cause, and safety incidents.

The Grievance Redressal Officer shall acknowledge every complaint within twenty-four hours of receipt, and shall resolve every complaint within a period not exceeding fifteen days.

The driver-partner may escalate any unresolved complaint to the appropriate State Transport Authority.

# Fare and commission transparency

An aggregator shall display, at the time of accepting a trip request, the estimated fare to the customer and the estimated payout to the driver-partner.

An aggregator shall not vary the actual payout from the estimated payout by more than a factor prescribed by the State Government, save for justifiable and disclosed reasons (route deviation initiated by the customer, wait time, tolls, cancellations).

The aggregator's commission shall not exceed twenty per cent of the applicable fare.

# Deactivation and re-engagement

An aggregator shall not deactivate the account of a driver-partner without providing at least seven days' written notice and an opportunity to be heard, except in cases of established safety violations or established fraud.

Every deactivation notice shall state the specific reason for the deactivation and the appeal path.
