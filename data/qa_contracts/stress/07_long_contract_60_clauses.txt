MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Aggregator platform × Delivery Partner
Effective: 1 January 2026
Version 4.2

BETWEEN:
MegaDeliver India Pvt. Ltd., 202 DLF Cyber City, Sector 25, Gurugram 122002 ("MDI")
AND
The Registered Delivery Partner ("DP")

RECITALS
A. MDI operates an online food-delivery aggregator platform in India.
B. The DP wishes to be onboarded to the MDI Platform for the purpose of delivering food orders.
C. The parties wish to record the terms of their engagement.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Platform" means the mobile and web application operated by MDI.
1.2 "Order" means a request from a customer for delivery of food from a merchant partner.
1.3 "Zone" means the geographic delivery zone assigned to the DP.
1.4 "Cash on Delivery" or "COD" means an order where the customer pays in cash on delivery.
1.5 "Payout" means the amount payable to the DP for services rendered.
1.6 "Rate Card" means the published schedule of per-order payouts.
1.7 "Onboarding" means the process of activating a DP account on the Platform.
1.8 "Deactivation" means the suspension of DP access to the Platform.

2. RELATIONSHIP

2.1 The DP is an independent contractor.
2.2 No employer-employee relationship is created.
2.3 The DP shall not be entitled to Provident Fund, ESIC, gratuity, or paid leave.
2.4 The DP acknowledges the freedom to accept or decline any Order.
2.5 The DP acknowledges the freedom to log off the Platform at any time.

3. ONBOARDING

3.1 The DP shall submit valid identity proof, driving licence, and vehicle documents.
3.2 MDI shall conduct background verification.
3.3 The DP shall attend a one-day orientation programme.
3.4 The DP shall accept the Onboarding fee of Rs 500, deducted from the first payout.
3.5 The DP shall deposit a refundable security amount of Rs 2,000.

4. RATE AND PAYOUT

4.1 MDI shall publish the Rate Card on the Platform from time to time.
4.2 MDI reserves the right to modify the Rate Card at its sole discretion.
4.3 Modifications shall take effect on publication and require no separate notice.
4.4 The DP waives any objection to Rate Card changes.
4.5 Payout shall be credited weekly to the DP's registered bank account.
4.6 Payout is subject to deduction of taxes, penalties, cancellation charges, and disputed order refunds.
4.7 MDI's determination of any deduction shall be final and binding.

5. WORKING HOURS

5.1 The DP is free to log in and log off at any time.
5.2 MDI recommends the DP take breaks after long stretches.
5.3 MDI does not guarantee any minimum Orders per hour.

6. VEHICLE

6.1 The DP shall use their own two-wheeler.
6.2 The DP shall maintain the vehicle at their own cost.
6.3 The DP shall carry valid registration and insurance.

7. INSURANCE

7.1 The DP shall arrange comprehensive motor insurance at their own cost.
7.2 The DP shall arrange personal accident insurance at their own cost.
7.3 MDI does not provide health, life, or accident insurance.

8. PERFORMANCE STANDARDS

8.1 The DP shall accept at least 70% of Orders offered.
8.2 The DP shall maintain a customer rating of at least 4.2 out of 5.
8.3 The DP shall complete Orders within the estimated delivery time.
8.4 MDI may deactivate the DP for falling below any performance standard.

9. UNIFORM AND APPEARANCE

9.1 The DP shall wear the MDI T-shirt during working hours.
9.2 The DP shall carry the MDI delivery bag at all times.
9.3 Cost of uniform and bag is deducted from the first Payout.

10. TERMINATION

10.1 Either party may terminate this agreement at any time without notice.
10.2 MDI may deactivate the DP account immediately for any policy violation.
10.3 No hearing, appeal, or reinstatement right is available.
10.4 On termination, MDI shall pay the pending Payout after deductions within 30 days.

11. INDEMNITY

11.1 The DP shall indemnify MDI against any and all claims arising from the DP's services.
11.2 The indemnity is unlimited in amount and scope.
11.3 The indemnity survives termination of this agreement.

12. LIABILITY LIMITATION

12.1 MDI's aggregate liability to the DP shall not exceed Rs 5,000.
12.2 MDI shall not be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

13. NON-COMPETE

13.1 During the term of engagement and for six months after termination, the DP shall not deliver for a competing platform.
13.2 Breach entitles MDI to injunctive relief and damages of Rs 25,000.

14. CONFIDENTIALITY

14.1 The DP shall not disclose any Platform data, customer data, or business information.
14.2 This obligation survives termination.

15. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

15.1 MDI owns all intellectual property in the Platform, brand, and content.
15.2 The DP is granted a limited licence to use the Platform.

16. DATA

16.1 The DP consents to collection, use, and sharing of DP data by MDI.
16.2 Data may be shared with MDI's affiliates, service providers, and business partners.
16.3 The DP consents to the processing of personal data in accordance with MDI's privacy policy.

17. TAX

17.1 The DP is responsible for filing income tax returns.
17.2 The DP is responsible for GST registration if turnover exceeds the threshold.
17.3 MDI shall deduct TDS as required by law.

18. FORCE MAJEURE

18.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform due to events beyond reasonable control.

19. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

19.1 Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration.
19.2 The seat of arbitration shall be Mumbai.
19.3 The language of arbitration shall be English.
19.4 The arbitrator shall be appointed by MDI.
19.5 The decision of the arbitrator shall be final.
19.6 The DP waives any right to class or collective action.

20. GOVERNING LAW

20.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of India.
20.2 Courts of Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

21. AMENDMENT

21.1 MDI may amend this agreement at any time by publishing the updated version on the Platform.
21.2 Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the amendment.

22. WAIVER OF STATUTORY RIGHTS

22.1 The DP acknowledges that the DP is not covered by the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948.
22.2 The DP acknowledges that the DP is not covered by the Payment of Wages Act, 1936.
22.3 The DP acknowledges that the DP is not covered by the Minimum Wages Act, 1948.
22.4 The DP waives any claim to gratuity under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.

23. ANTI-CORRUPTION

23.1 The DP shall not offer or accept any bribe or improper payment.

24. NOTICES

24.1 All notices shall be sent by email to the address registered on the Platform.

25. ASSIGNMENT

25.1 The DP shall not assign or transfer this agreement.
25.2 MDI may assign this agreement to any affiliate.

26. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

26.1 This agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties.

27. SEVERABILITY

27.1 If any provision is held unenforceable, the rest of the agreement remains in effect.

28. ACCEPTANCE

28.1 By clicking "Accept" on the Platform, the DP acknowledges having read and agreed to this agreement.
