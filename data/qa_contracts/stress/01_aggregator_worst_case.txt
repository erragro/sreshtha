DELIVERY PARTNER AGREEMENT

This agreement is executed on the 15th day of March, 2026 at Bengaluru, Karnataka between:

1. QuickBites Delivery Pvt. Ltd., a company having its registered office at Prestige Tower, Marathahalli, Bengaluru 560037 (hereinafter referred to as "the Company")

2. Priyanka Sharma, resident of 12/4 MG Road, Bengaluru 560001 (hereinafter referred to as "the Delivery Partner")

1. NATURE OF RELATIONSHIP

1.1 The Delivery Partner is an independent contractor and NOT an employee of the Company. This agreement does not create any employer-employee relationship between the parties. The Delivery Partner shall not claim any statutory benefits including Provident Fund, ESIC, gratuity, or paid leave.

1.2 The Delivery Partner acknowledges and agrees that no principal-agent, joint venture, or franchise relationship exists between the parties.

2. SERVICES

2.1 The Delivery Partner shall accept and complete orders assigned through the Company's application platform, using their own vehicle.

2.2 The Delivery Partner shall bear all costs of fuel, vehicle maintenance, insurance premiums, mobile data, and any other operating expenses incurred in performing the services.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Company shall pay the Delivery Partner as per the rate card published on the platform from time to time. The Company reserves the right to modify the rate card, including reducing the per-order payout, at its sole and absolute discretion, without prior notice.

3.2 Payments shall be made weekly by bank transfer, subject to deduction of any fees, penalties, or claims determined by the Company.

3.3 The Delivery Partner waives any claim to minimum wage, overtime, or any other payment beyond the amounts stated in the rate card.

4. TERMINATION

4.1 Either party may terminate this agreement at any time, with or without cause, without any prior notice or reason.

4.2 Notwithstanding the above, the Company may immediately deactivate the Delivery Partner's account in case of any policy violation, customer complaint, or fraud, without notice, hearing, or opportunity to appeal.

5. INDEMNIFICATION

5.1 The Delivery Partner shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, and expenses (including legal fees) arising from or connected with the performance of services under this agreement.

5.2 This indemnity shall be unlimited and shall survive the termination of this agreement.

6. NON-COMPETE AND EXCLUSIVITY

6.1 During the term of this agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following termination for any reason, the Delivery Partner shall not directly or indirectly provide delivery services to any competing platform operating in India.

6.2 Breach of this clause shall entitle the Company to injunctive relief and liquidated damages of Rs 50,000.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION

7.1 This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

7.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration in Mumbai, Maharashtra, in the English language, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, before a sole arbitrator appointed by the Company.

7.3 The seat of arbitration shall be Mumbai and the courts of Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA

8.1 The Delivery Partner consents to the collection, storage, use, and sharing of their personal data, location data, earnings data, ratings, and performance metrics by the Company with any third party for any business purpose the Company deems appropriate.

9. GENERAL

9.1 The Delivery Partner shall wear the uniform provided by the Company and shall maintain the vehicle in good condition at their own cost.

9.2 This agreement supersedes all prior understandings between the parties.
