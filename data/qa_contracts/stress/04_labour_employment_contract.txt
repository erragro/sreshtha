EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 12 January 2026 at Chennai, Tamil Nadu:

BETWEEN

Trichy Textile Mills Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the Companies Act 2013, having its registered office at Plot 42, MEPZ SEZ, Chennai 600045 (hereinafter "the Employer")

AND

Meenakshi Ravi, adult, resident of 8/2 Anna Nagar, Trichy 620001 (hereinafter "the Employee")

WHEREAS the Employer wishes to engage the Employee in the position of Textile Operator, and the Employee wishes to accept such engagement, the parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE I — POSITION AND DUTIES

1.1 The Employee shall serve as a Textile Operator at the Employer's Trichy manufacturing unit.

1.2 The Employee shall report to the Shift Supervisor and shall perform the duties assigned to the position, including operation of weaving machinery, quality inspection, and compliance with safety protocols.

1.3 The Employee shall be a full-time employee of the Employer within the meaning of the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946.

ARTICLE II — TERM

2.1 This Agreement shall commence on 15 January 2026 and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Article VII.

2.2 The Employee shall be on probation for the first six (6) months, during which either party may terminate on fifteen (15) days' written notice.

ARTICLE III — REMUNERATION

3.1 The Employee shall be paid a monthly gross salary of Rs 22,000 (Rupees Twenty-Two Thousand only), payable by the 7th of the following month.

3.2 The salary shall be subject to statutory deductions including Employees' Provident Fund (EPF), Employees' State Insurance (ESI), and Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) as applicable.

3.3 The Employer shall contribute the employer's share of EPF at twelve percent (12%) of basic salary in accordance with the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.

3.4 The Employer shall register the Employee with the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) and shall contribute the employer's share as required under the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948.

ARTICLE IV — HOURS AND LEAVE

4.1 Working hours shall be forty-eight (48) hours per week, in eight-hour shifts, six days per week, in accordance with the Factories Act, 1948.

4.2 The Employee shall be entitled to fifteen (15) days of earned leave per year, seven (7) days of casual leave, and twelve (12) days of sick leave.

4.3 Female employees shall be entitled to maternity benefit as provided under the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961.

ARTICLE V — GRATUITY

5.1 On termination of employment after five (5) or more years of continuous service, the Employee shall be entitled to gratuity as computed under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.

ARTICLE VI — CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Employee shall maintain the confidentiality of all trade secrets, customer information, and proprietary processes of the Employer, both during and for two (2) years after employment.

ARTICLE VII — TERMINATION

7.1 After completion of probation, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice.

7.2 The Employer may terminate the Employee for misconduct in accordance with the Standing Orders of the establishment, following the enquiry procedure prescribed under Section 33 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.

7.3 The Employee may raise any industrial dispute in accordance with Section 2A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.

ARTICLE VIII — GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

8.1 The Employer shall constitute a Grievance Redressal Committee under Section 9C of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.

8.2 The Employer has an Internal Committee constituted under Section 4 of the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. Complaints may be filed with the Presiding Officer, Mrs. Kavitha Rangarajan (kavitha.rangarajan@trichytextile.com).

ARTICLE IX — GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India applicable to employment in Tamil Nadu.

9.2 The Labour Courts of Chennai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

Signed at Chennai on 12 January 2026.

For Trichy Textile Mills Pvt. Ltd.        The Employee
Managing Director                          Meenakshi Ravi
