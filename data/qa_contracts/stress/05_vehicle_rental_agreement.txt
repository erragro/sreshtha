TWO-WHEELER RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 20 February 2026 at Delhi.

BETWEEN:
Speedy Wheels Rentals LLP, GST No. 07AABCS1429A1Z5, having its office at 118 Karol Bagh, New Delhi 110005 (hereinafter "the Lessor")

AND:
Vikram Singh, holder of Aadhaar No. XXXX-XXXX-1234 and DL No. DL-020200234-2019, resident of Sector 15, Rohini, New Delhi 110085 (hereinafter "the Lessee")

WHEREAS the Lessor is engaged in the business of renting two-wheeler motorcycles to individual delivery-partners, and the Lessee wishes to lease a Bajaj Pulsar 150 (Registration DL 8S CX 5471) for the purpose of performing delivery work on a food-delivery platform:

1. LEASE PERIOD

1.1 The lease shall commence on 25 February 2026 and continue until 24 August 2026, unless earlier terminated in accordance with clause 8.

1.2 On expiry of the lease period, the Lessee shall return the vehicle to the Lessor's Karol Bagh location by 5:00 PM on the last day, failing which a per-day penalty of Rs 500 shall accrue.

2. RENTAL AND DEPOSIT

2.1 The Lessee shall pay a monthly rental of Rs 8,500, in advance, on or before the 25th of each month.

2.2 The Lessee shall pay a security deposit of Rs 15,000 at the time of taking possession of the vehicle. This deposit is non-refundable in case of early termination initiated by the Lessee.

2.3 The Lessor may adjust the security deposit against damages, unpaid rentals, or traffic challans at the Lessor's sole discretion, and any shortfall shall be paid by the Lessee within seven days of demand.

3. VEHICLE CONDITION AND MAINTENANCE

3.1 The Lessee accepts the vehicle in its present condition. A vehicle inspection report attached as Annexure A shall be signed by both parties.

3.2 The Lessee shall bear all costs of routine maintenance including engine oil changes, tyre replacement, brake pad replacement, and any repairs whatsoever.

3.3 Depreciation of the vehicle over the lease period shall be recovered from the Lessee by deducting Rs 12,000 from the security deposit at the end of the lease.

4. INSURANCE

4.1 The vehicle is registered in the name of the Lessor and carries third-party motor insurance as required under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.

4.2 The Lessee shall obtain and maintain, at their own cost, comprehensive insurance covering own damage, theft, and personal-accident cover during the lease period.

4.3 In the event of any accident, the Lessee shall be responsible for the insurance excess, deductible, and any depreciation on the claim payout.

5. USE OF VEHICLE

5.1 The Lessee shall use the vehicle solely for the personal purpose of performing delivery services on a platform. Sub-letting is strictly prohibited.

5.2 The Lessee shall obey all traffic rules and shall be personally responsible for all traffic challans, fines, and penalties.

5.3 The Lessee shall not use the vehicle to transport any passenger, cargo, or goods other than food-delivery consignments.

6. FUEL AND CONSUMABLES

6.1 The Lessee shall bear all fuel costs.

7. WARRANTY DISCLAIMER

7.1 The Lessor makes no warranty as to the fitness of the vehicle for any particular purpose. The Lessee accepts the vehicle "AS IS".

8. TERMINATION

8.1 Either party may terminate this agreement on fifteen (15) days' written notice.

8.2 The Lessor may repossess the vehicle without notice in the event of any default of rental payment exceeding seven (7) days.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Any dispute arising under this agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Delhi.
